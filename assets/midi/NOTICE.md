# Demonstration MIDI recordings

Two recordings of piano performances by competitors of the International Piano-e-Competition, played on a Yamaha Disklavier. They are taken
**unchanged** from the MAESTRO dataset v3.0.0 (<https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/maestro>):

| File | Piece | MAESTRO file |
|---|---|---|
| `liszt-un-sospiro-2011.mid` | Franz Liszt, *Un Sospiro*, Concert Etude No. 3 in D-flat major, S. 144/3 (2011) | `2011/MIDI-Unprocessed_04_R3_2011_MID--AUDIO_R3-D2_03_Track03_wav.midi` |
| `liszt-la-campanella-2013.mid` | Franz Liszt, *La Campanella*, Grandes etudes de Paganini No. 3, S. 141/3 (2013) | `2013/ORIG-MIDI_01_7_6_13_Group__MID--AUDIO_02_R1_2013_wav--5.midi` |
