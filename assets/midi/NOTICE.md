# liszt-un-sospiro-2011.mid

A recording of a piano performance (Franz Liszt, *Un Sospiro*, Concert Etude No. 3 in D-flat major, S. 144/3),
played by a competitor of the International Piano-e-Competition in 2011 on a Yamaha Disklavier.
The file is taken **unchanged** from the MAESTRO dataset v3.0.0
(<https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/maestro>, file `2011/MIDI-Unprocessed_04_R3_2011_MID--AUDIO_R3-D2_03_Track03_wav.midi`).
